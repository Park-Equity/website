Leadership

The people behind Park Equity

Based in the New York City metropolitan area, working with owners, operators and founders across North America.

BH

Brett Harwood

Founder, Principal and Philanthropist

Brett founded Park Equity in 2002 after more than 25 years leading parking businesses, including two decades as President of Square Industries and terms as President and Chairman of the National Parking Association. He is Chairman and Co-Founder of Noson and OnAir Parking, Chairman of Welcome Parking and a longtime partner in Pi Alley Garage.

Brett is a dedicated philanthropist, and his life's mission is to leave the world better than we found it. A former Trustee of Franklin & Marshall College, he also served as Chairman and Trustee of Liberty Healthcare System.

SH

Shawn Harwood

Managing Partner

Shawn is CEO of Square Parking and a fourth generation leader in the parking industry. He joined Park Equity in 2021, after serving as Director of Development for Square Parking, Pi Alley Garage and Welcome Parking, and as Senior Operations Manager at SP+.

An NPA 40 Under 40 honoree and an active voice with the National Parking Association and IPMI, Shawn brings hands on operating experience across office, residential, hospital, hotel, stadium and garage environments.

Joe Suri

Executive Advisor

Joe brings a long career in parking and mobility to Park Equity's portfolio and advisory work. He is Director of Business Development at Square Parking, previously worked at SKIDATA and founded NextGen Mobility Reps.
